The list would contain the possibilities from a "c" of 2 up to a "c" equal to "n". So for example if i have a "n" of 3 i can have a "c" of 2 and 3. And the frequencies would correspond to:
    - For a "c" of 2 and "n" of 3 i can sample 1 item from a class and two items from another class. So the only proportions i would always observe for this case is 1/3 and 2/3
    - For a "c" of 3 and "n" of 3 i can sample 1 item from each class "c". So the only proportions i would always observe for this case are 1/3, 1/3 and 1/3. 

So the resulting list for the case n=3 is : [[[1/3,2/3]],[[1/3,1/3,1/3]]]

The resulting list for the case of n=4 is : [[[0.25,0.75],[0.5,0.5]],[[0.25,0.25,0.5]],[[0.25,0.25,0.25,0.25]]]
